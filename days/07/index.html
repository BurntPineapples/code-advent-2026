The Neural Painter

A tiny neural network that has never seen a picture is learning to paint one, right now, in your browser.

The picture (paint on it!)

What the network paints

Inside its head: 64 neurons

What each hidden neuron reacts to.
Together they add up to the painting.

The network never stores a single pixel. It's a function: give it an (x, y) position and it guesses a colour. Every frame it guesses a few hundred random pixels, checks how wrong it was, and nudges its 6,000 weights a little. Turn Fourier features off to see why modern networks need them, and paint on the left to watch it re-learn.
